How to pay with Remitly

Remitly is an app and website used to send money internationally.

  1. Open Remitly using the button below.
  2. Select Cameroon as the destination country.
  3. Choose Mobile Money and select MTN Mobile Money.
  4. Enter the recipient details shown above, then review everything before sending.
Open Remitly →

Don't have the Remitly app? You can use Remitly in your browser or download the app.

⚠️ Before sending, double-check: Cameroon · Mobile Money · MTN Mobile Money · recipient name · recipient phone number.
