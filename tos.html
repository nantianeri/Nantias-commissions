Commission rules.

This is the initial TOS structure. We can refine every section before launch.

General

The artist reserves the right to accept or decline any request. A submitted request is not a purchase.

Payment

Payment information is provided only after acceptance. Payment is made externally. Payment must be verified before the commission is considered officially confirmed.

Process & Revisions

Minor changes are expected during the sketch stage. Major changes after approval may incur an additional fee.

Refunds

Refund rules will be finalized before launch and should clearly explain what happens at each stage of the commission.

Usage

Personal-use and commercial-use rights are separate. Commercial rights may cost extra.

Portfolio Rights

The artist may display completed commissioned artwork on social media and in their portfolio unless another agreement is made.

Before accepting real clients, replace these preliminary rules with your final policies.
