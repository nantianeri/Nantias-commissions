About Me

Hi, I'm Nantia Neri!

I'm a digital artist and illustrator behind Nantia's Art.

I love creating characters, creatures, fantasy artwork, fan art, and illustrations inspired by the things I enjoy. Drawing is a way for me to explore ideas, experiment with different styles, and bring characters and worlds to life.

Alongside my personal artwork, I also take commissions, where I get to create something specifically for someone else based on their ideas and preferences.

One of my aspirations for the future is to become an animator. I would love to bring my drawings and characters to life through animation, and I hope that one day I can achieve that dream.

This website is my little corner of the internet where I can share my artwork, showcase my portfolio, and make commissioning artwork a little easier.

Thank you for stopping by and supporting my art!
